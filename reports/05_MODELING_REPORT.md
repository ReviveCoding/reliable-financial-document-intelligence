# Modeling Report

The selected candidate `P0_rfdi_deterministic_v1` combines anchored B0 extraction, ambiguity-safe normalization, reconciliation, criticality-weighted risk, review/quarantine routing, strict schema, and provenance/state controls. Thresholds were frozen before final evaluation.

No OCR, line-item extraction, relation encoder, field fusion, deep classifier, synthetic scaling, or cross-dataset model was trained. Their phases are blocked or skipped with reason in `WORKFLOW_STATE.json`.
