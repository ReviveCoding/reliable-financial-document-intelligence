# Selective automation

At 50% review, R0 captures 75.6% of critical errors and leaves 20.0% critical risk among accepted documents. R4 captures 70.7% and leaves 24.0%. Neither satisfies the frozen operating gate at or below 50% review.

Even R0 at 80% review retains 15.0% critical false-accept risk while capturing 92.7%. Therefore v3.2 does not recommend an automatic-accept threshold. The normalized-cost column reuses illustrative v3.1 weights (review=1; unsafe critical acceptance=25), not institutional costs.
