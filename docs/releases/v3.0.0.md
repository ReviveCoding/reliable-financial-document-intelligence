# R-FDI v3.0.0 — Reliable Financial Document Intelligence

R-FDI v3.0.0 completes a three-stage, evidence-governed research program for
financial document intelligence. The release decision is `V3_EXTENSION_SUCCESS`.
This is a production-style research simulation, not a live bank deployment or a
regulatory-certification claim.

## Research lineage

- **V1 — governance foundation:** validated fail-closed workflow, evidence, risk,
  routing, and audit mechanics. Its historical `NO_PROMOTION` decision is retained.
- **V2 — real-document and CUDA study:** evaluated fixed model families on
  authorized public documents. The offline research candidate earned `PROMOTE`
  after all ten frozen gates passed.
- **V3 — production hardening:** executed runtime, persistence, reviewer, load,
  recovery, security, Docker GPU, and accelerated-serving studies. All eight
  frozen extension requirements passed.

## Measured highlights

- Donut CORD locked-test leaf F1: **0.8372**; total exact: **0.9895**.
- PP-OCRv5 + rules CORD total exact: **0.4632**.
- Paired total-exact gain: **+0.5263**, bootstrap 95% CI **[+0.4316, +0.6211]**.
- LayoutLMv3 FUNSD locked-test macro-F1: **0.7009**.
- PaddleOCR-VL on the same 20 CORD development documents: native mean latency
  **20.403603 s** versus Docker/vLLM **10.726820 s**, or **1.902111x** mean
  speedup. Served content-presence recall was **0.917128**.

## Runtime and reliability evidence

- Real Compose services verified for API, worker, reviewer, Redis, PostgreSQL,
  MinIO, and MLflow.
- MLflow metadata persisted in PostgreSQL and its matching artifact in MinIO.
- API/reviewer behavior passed 7/7 checks.
- Local executable fault study passed 7/7; infrastructure stop/restart recovery
  passed 3/3 for Redis, PostgreSQL, and MinIO.
- Docker exposed one RTX 4090 Laptop GPU; the bounded PaddleOCR-VL server retained
  at least 35.51% observed VRAM headroom.
- The 24-case adversarial benchmark preserved all boundaries and capability
  isolation. Detector misses and benign activations remain explicit.

## Explicit limitations

PaddleOCR-VL figures are development content-presence measurements, not official
structured CORD F1 or locked-final results. The application uses a SQLite durable
queue and filesystem object store; Redis is not yet the active job transport.
DocILE and REFinD require human-authorized access. Textract was skipped without a
positive AWS budget and valid credentials. Compose credentials are local defaults.
No paid cloud, production-bank, regulatory-compliance, or multi-GPU claim is made.
