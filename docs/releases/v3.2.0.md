# R-FDI v3.2.0 — Critical Risk Control & Line-Item Reliability

R-FDI v3.2.0 extends the frozen v3.0 model/runtime baseline and v3.1 analytics with row-aware line-item evaluation, production-safe risk features, grouped model selection, selective-risk analysis, and finite-sample certification.

## Scientific scope

- Extractor weights did not change.
- CORD test is a `RETROSPECTIVE_LOCKED_BENCHMARK`, not a fresh untouched holdout.
- V1 `NO_PROMOTION`, V2 `PROMOTE`, V3 `V3_EXTENSION_SUCCESS`, and v3.1 `V3_1_ANALYSIS_SUCCESS` remain unchanged.
- Promotion in this extension could only mean shadow risk routing—not autonomous production action.

## Measured findings

- After methodology correction, path-occurrence field F1 is 0.8016, positional-row field F1 is 0.7887, permutation-invariant matched-row F1 is 0.9534, and matched-field micro F1 is 0.8585. Row exact match is 0.6023. Matched-row F1 is not a field-accuracy metric.
- Benign permutation-only prevalence is 0.0% in the retrospective sample; semantic row-association errors affect 10.0%. Benign permutations are excluded from monetary risk.
- The corrected broadly defined critical monetary-error prevalence is 41.0%; line-item critical-error prevalence is 33.0%.
- Raw confidence remains the best evaluated risk ranker: retrospective AURC 0.2356, PR-AUC 0.8200, and AUROC 0.8189.
- The best learned candidate, shallow gradient boosting R4, records AURC 0.3314, PR-AUC 0.5754, and AUROC 0.6554; it does not improve on raw confidence.
- At 50% review, raw confidence captures 75.6% of critical errors and leaves 20.0% critical risk among accepted documents. R4 captures 70.7% and leaves 24.0%.
- The already-observed certification partition was replayed: 5% has insufficient support and 10%/20% are uncertified. These statistics are non-confirmatory and promotion-ineligible.
- High occlusion remains the largest controlled extraction degradation. The learned risk response is weak and inconsistent when only production-safe image descriptors vary.

## Decision

`V3_2_RISK_MODEL_NO_PROMOTION`

The existing runtime and action semantics remain unchanged. The corrected-target R4 replay is retained as reproducible offline negative evidence. Corrected replay cannot justify promotion without genuinely fresh external evidence.

## Limitations

This is research reliability evidence, not a live bank deployment, regulatory certification, or production-risk guarantee. CORD test and the certification partition had already been observed before correction. The robustness response reuses a 10-document development cohort and holds clean post-extraction features fixed. Authorized DocILE LIR evidence and a larger fresh independent certification sample remain future work.
