# R-FDI v3.2.0 — Critical Risk Control & Line-Item Reliability

R-FDI v3.2.0 extends the frozen v3.0 model/runtime baseline and v3.1 analytics with row-aware line-item evaluation, production-safe risk features, grouped model selection, selective-risk analysis, and finite-sample certification analysis.

## Scope

- Extractor weights did not change.
- V3.2 is a risk-control and line-item reliability extension; it does not retrain the extractor.
- CORD test is a `RETROSPECTIVE_LOCKED_BENCHMARK`, not a fresh untouched holdout.
- V1 `NO_PROMOTION`, V2 `PROMOTE`, V3 `V3_EXTENSION_SUCCESS`, and v3.1 `V3_1_ANALYSIS_SUCCESS` remain unchanged.
- Promotion in this extension could only mean shadow risk routing—not autonomous production action.

## Methodology correction

Before public v3.2 publication, an audit found that E0 did not implement the intended historical comparison, benign row permutations were conflated with semantic row-association failures, and row matchability was named as though it represented field accuracy. The evaluator was corrected to report these concepts separately, and monetary-risk labels and all dependent risk evidence were regenerated. The pre-correction audit and methodology history remain preserved in the repository.

## Corrected metrics

- E0 path-occurrence field F1 is 0.8016; E1 positional-row field F1 is 0.7887.
- E2 permutation-invariant matched-row F1 is 0.9534 and measures row matchability, not field accuracy. E2 matched-field micro-F1 is 0.8585 and row exact rate is 0.6023.
- Benign permutation-only prevalence is 0.0% in the retrospective sample; semantic row-association errors affect 10.0%. Benign permutations are excluded from monetary risk.
- The corrected broadly defined critical monetary-error prevalence is 41.0%; line-item critical-error prevalence is 33.0%.
- Raw confidence remains the best evaluated risk ranker: retrospective AURC 0.2356, PR-AUC 0.8200, and AUROC 0.8189.
- The best learned candidate, shallow gradient boosting R4, records AURC 0.3314, PR-AUC 0.5754, and AUROC 0.6554; it does not improve on raw confidence.
- At 50% review, raw confidence captures 75.6% of critical errors and leaves 20.0% critical risk among accepted documents. R4 captures 70.7% and leaves 24.0%.
- At 80% review, raw confidence captures 92.7% of critical errors and leaves 15.0% critical risk among accepted documents.
- High occlusion remains the largest controlled extraction degradation. The learned risk response is weak and inconsistent when only production-safe image descriptors vary.

## Risk decision

`V3_2_RISK_MODEL_NO_PROMOTION`

R0 raw confidence remains stronger than R4: it has lower AURC and higher PR-AUC/AUROC, and it captures more critical errors with lower accepted critical risk at 50% review.

## Certification

The already-observed certification partition was replayed: 5% has insufficient support and 10%/20% are uncertified. Because the evidence was reused after correction, this replay is non-confirmatory and promotion-ineligible; it is not fresh certification evidence.

## Runtime

API and action semantics were not changed. Runtime routing remains unchanged, and the corrected-target R4 replay is retained as reproducible offline negative evidence.

## Limitations

- This is a research portfolio, not a live bank deployment, regulatory certification, or production-risk guarantee.
- Only one physical GPU was used; no multi-GPU execution is claimed.
- No fresh DocILE LIR validation was available.
- CORD test and the certification partition had already been observed before correction.
- The robustness response reuses a 10-document development cohort and holds clean post-extraction features fixed.
- Fresh external line-item evidence and a genuinely fresh independent certification sample are required before reconsidering risk-model promotion.
