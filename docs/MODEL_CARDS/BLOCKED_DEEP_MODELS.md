# Model Card — Blocked Deep Families

LayoutLMv3, Donut, PaddleOCR-VL-1.6, and Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct were verified as relevant candidates but were not loaded or run. Active WSL could not initialize NVML and PyTorch was absent. In accordance with GPU-first policy, no long CPU substitute was performed. No accuracy, latency, memory, or cost claim is made for these families.
